# MPPG 5.b commissioning plan specifications

In the HyperIO commissioning app, choose **Open…** and load [commissioning-mppg5b-plan-set.json](../examples/commissioning-mppg5b-plan-set.json).
The worksheet supplies proposed beam setups, source datasets, measurement endpoints and baseline selections for the numbered validation tests and routine QA.
The worked inventory assumes a conventional linac with 6 MV flattened photons, MLC and jaw collimation, and representative 9 MeV electrons.
Use **Save as…** for the local copy and expand it using the actual commissioned inventory.
Use **Build Monaco patient…** to stage a native patient with separate plans for cases assigned matching saved Monaco templates.
The templates supply the machine, beam geometry, MU and phantom studysets.
See the [application instructions](../applications/README.md#mppg-5b-commissioning-worksheet) for staging and copying the patient folder into the clinic.

## Apply the inventory

| Guideline section | Apply the cases to | Local inputs that determine the plans |
| --- | --- | --- |
| §5 photon model and basic checks | Each distinct configured photon beam and applicable algorithm, including distinct FFF/SRS models and physical wedges where used. The modelling-module comparison in 5.2 applies when that module is available and is performed once per algorithm. Add 5.9 for each available nonphysical wedge angle. | Actual calibration geometry and output, minimum clinical static field, usable field and leaf travel, shortest clinical SSD, and available wedge directions and sizes. |
| §6 heterogeneity | Each image source used for dose calculation in 6.1, including applicable on-board imaging, and each photon beam energy in 6.2. | Scanner and protocol, commissioned CT-to-density or material table, insert specifications, physical slab composition, detector locations and scatter conditions. |
| §7 modulated delivery | Validate each intended delivery technique and clinical energy. Use the static small-aperture checks to represent the jaw and MLC conditions of the intended techniques. The external review guidance allows internal extensions to other energies and algorithms after appropriate validation of an initial combination on the same TPS. | Delivered jaw configuration, local optimisation settings, clinical dose grid and VMAT angular spacing, clinically representative image sets, and QA and end-to-end phantom availability. |
| §8 electrons | Reference output, custom cutout and obliquity for each commissioned energy. Include standard-cone PDDs and output factors for all energy/cone combinations. Use at least one energy and a suitable comparison depth for the heterogeneity case, and verify any additional nonroutine setups used clinically. | Electron calibration depth and cone, standard and extended SSD, actual cutout drawing, lateral scatter equilibrium and insert geometry appropriate for the electron range. |
| §9 routine QA | Archive representative static, wedge and modulated photon plans for the configured beams where applicable, and a heterogeneous, curved-surface electron plan per energy. Include extended SSD and bolus verification as applicable. | Commissioned baseline plan and dose, TPS and beam-model identity, saved calculation settings and software/configuration checksums. |

[AAPM MPPG 5.b, §§5–9](https://doi.org/10.1002/acm2.13641)

## Build the plans and acquire the evidence

1. For 5.1 and 8.1, copy the locally measured calibration setup into the TPS. Keep the reference dose per MU, field definition, SSD or SAD geometry and measurement depth together. For 5.3, use matching measured commissioning data with the same geometry and normalisation as the calculation.
2. For the static photon cases, interpret dimensions at the stated field plane. Translate signed beam's-eye-view jaw and MLC positions into the TPS and machine conventions. For oblique beams, verify SSD along the central axis to its intersection with the phantom and compare measured and calculated samples along matching paths. Record the measured depth near and beyond dmax for each energy and choose scan ranges that cover the high-dose, penumbra and tail regions. Preserve the calculation grid separately from the image spacing. [MPPG 5.b, Tables 4–6](https://doi.org/10.1002/acm2.13641)
3. For 6.1, acquire the density phantom on each applicable imaging system and record known insert values with the corresponding TPS reports. For 6.2, match the physical slab stack and central-axis detector positions in the TPS, then compare the same distal-to-proximal dose ratio in measurement and calculation, with both points away from interfaces and buildup/builddown. The worksheet's slab dimensions come from the worked example in the guideline. The exact sample depths depend on the physical stack and beam. [MPPG 5.b, §6](https://doi.org/10.1002/acm2.13641)
4. For 7.3, import the official TG-119 phantom CT and structures for mock head and neck and C-shape, then use the TG-119 instructions and objectives to optimise and measure each plan. For 7.4, the worksheet selects AAPM Case 3 (right-upper-lobe lung) and Case 5 (head and neck SIB). Import their CT and structures, use the objective PDFs in the packages and plan with the local clinical settings. Create the verification plans on the QA phantom and retain absolute point-dose and distribution results where appropriate. [TG-119 dataset](https://zenodo.org/records/8037934), [TG-119 instructions, C3 and C4](https://www.aapm.org/pubs/tg119/TG119_Instructions_102109.pdf), [AAPM clinical validation datasets](https://www.aapm.org/pubs/MPPG/TPS/)
5. For 7.5, use the acquired scans of the head-and-neck and heterogeneous thorax phantoms. Follow the review provider's planning and irradiation protocol, including its prescription and required submissions. Obtain independent qualified medical physicist review if a third-party mail-in evaluation is unavailable. [MPPG 5.b, §7.1](https://doi.org/10.1002/acm2.13641), [IROC head and neck phantom](https://irochouston.mdanderson.org/rpc/services/Anthropomorphic_%20Phantoms/Anth_HNP.htm), [IROC thorax-lung phantom](https://irochouston.mdanderson.org/rpc/Services/Anthropomorphic_%20Phantoms/Phantom_Thorax_lung.htm)
6. For 8.2 and 8.3, match the measured cutout or cone, clinical SSD and physical surface geometry in the TPS. For 8.4, select a depth that samples the effect of the local insert within the electron range and retain the chamber measurement or heterogeneity-corrected manual result. For the electron routine QA baseline, AAPM Case 6 provides an optional curved thorax dataset. [MPPG 5.b, §§8–9](https://doi.org/10.1002/acm2.13641), [AAPM thorax dataset](https://www.aapm.org/pubs/MPPG/TPS/)
7. Link each completed TPS plan and result to its worksheet case ID. Keep the machine and model identifiers, actual aperture and imaging geometry, dose calculation settings, reference measurement setup, dose normalisation, and the original dose export together. Record the review outcome and any deviations. For §9, archive the accepted reference calculation and use it for the annual and post-upgrade comparisons. [MPPG 5.b, §§9–10](https://doi.org/10.1002/acm2.13641)

For composite IMRT/VMAT plans, use the separate point-dose, distribution and end-to-end evaluation criteria in Table 9 and §7.2. Apply the small-field PDD and output comparisons to the static 7.1 and 7.2 cases. Record detector choice, applicable small-field correction and reference geometry before interpreting differences. [MPPG 5.b, §§3 and 7](https://doi.org/10.1002/acm2.13641)
